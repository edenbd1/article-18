Article 18

A fund whose lifecycle is enforced by the ledger, not attested by an administrator.

The three phases of an XRPL closed-ended vault are the three phases Article 18(1) of the ELTIF Regulation (EU) 2015/760 imposes on a European long-term fund. Where the law asks a manager to declare compliance and audits it afterward, here the ledger refuses the transaction.

The eligibility gate holds on the secondary market

An assessor issues a suitability credential; the fund declares a domain that accepts it; the private vault is bound to that domain. A non-credentialed account is refused by consensus — not just at deposit, but on any share transfer.
